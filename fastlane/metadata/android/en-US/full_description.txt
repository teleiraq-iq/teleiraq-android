TELEIRAQ is a Matrix messaging app for direct messages, group conversations, and voice and video calls. It connects to the TELEIRAQ homeserver and supports end-to-end encryption in encrypted rooms.

TELEIRAQ is an independent modified version of Element Classic Android, not an official Element release or endorsed by Element.
